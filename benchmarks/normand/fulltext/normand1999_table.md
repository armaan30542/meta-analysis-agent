# Specialist stroke care vs routine care: length of hospital stay (days)
(Normand, Stat Med 1999; distributed as metafor::dat.normand1999)

Edinburgh: specialist care mean length of stay 55 days (SD 47, n = 155); routine care mean 75 days (SD 64, n = 156).
Orpington-Mild: specialist care mean length of stay 27 days (SD 7, n = 31); routine care mean 29 days (SD 4, n = 32).
Orpington-Moderate: specialist care mean length of stay 64 days (SD 17, n = 75); routine care mean 119 days (SD 29, n = 71).
Orpington-Severe: specialist care mean length of stay 66 days (SD 20, n = 18); routine care mean 137 days (SD 48, n = 18).
Montreal-Home: specialist care mean length of stay 14 days (SD 8, n = 8); routine care mean 18 days (SD 11, n = 13).
Montreal-Transfer: specialist care mean length of stay 19 days (SD 7, n = 57); routine care mean 18 days (SD 4, n = 52).
Newcastle: specialist care mean length of stay 52 days (SD 45, n = 34); routine care mean 41 days (SD 34, n = 33).
Umea: specialist care mean length of stay 21 days (SD 16, n = 110); routine care mean 31 days (SD 27, n = 183).
Uppsala: specialist care mean length of stay 30 days (SD 27, n = 60); routine care mean 23 days (SD 20, n = 52).
